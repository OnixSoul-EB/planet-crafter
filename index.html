Prime

Prime — the tutorial planetBest first world. Huge map. 15 stages to 4 TTi
Pros
Ore on the ground. Ice everywhere. 27 gold crates. Full wreck + bunker tour
Caves for every rare. Warden keys. Easiest power and water.
Cons
Biggest walks. Lakes drown low loot. Easy to overbuild and brown out

Auto-Crafter — factory + money loopUnlock 2.50 GTi. 2 Osmium + Super Alloy rod. −155 kW. 8-slot output. 20 m sphere. Scans every 5 s. Recipe must already be unlocked
What it actually does
Picks ONE recipe. Pulls ingredients from any inventory or loose item inside the green 20 m globe — Supply/Demand does not matter to the crafter
Drones only restock those boxes. Green lines = in range. Vertical lockers count. Outdoor farms and algae in the globe count.
Set it once
Open crafter → pick recipe → watch the ingredient dots go green. Set the crafter itself to Supply the finished item
8 slots fill and it stops. Drones must empty it or you stand there like it’s 2022.
Ingredient lockers
One locker per ingredient, inside 20 m, Demand that one item, priority 3
T3 extractors Supply the same ore. Don’t put your build-stock inside this globe or the factory eats your next living-compartment iron.
Protect your build pile
Second locker OUTSIDE the 20 m globe. Demand the same commons at priority 5+
Wiki trick: high-priority Demand beyond range keeps a private stock the crafter cannot steal.
Money loop
Crafter Supplies Pulsar / Circuit Board / Fabric / Fusion cell → Trade Rocket Demands that item → auto-launch when full (~10 min round trip)
Shop list prices drift; ranking holds: Pulsar and boards beat rods. Fusion cells cost 3 Pulsar — selling Pulsar is usually faster tokens/hour. Fabric is the cheap silk loop.
Best cash recipes
Pulsar Quartz (osmium+zeolite+iridium+uranium+methane). Circuit Board (silicon+iron+Al+zeolite+N2+bioplastic). Fabric from silk gens
High-quality food / cookies if you already farm beans and wheat. Don’t auto-sell every rod — you still need them.
Build-stock recipes
Rods, rocket engines, drones T2/T3, water bottles, fertilizer — Supply a Demand locker labeled BUILD
One crafter per recipe. Cloning five pulsar crafters is how you fund T2 drones.
Power and fuses
Optimizer + production fuse on the cluster. Energy fuse on the grid. Trade fuse on the rocket
Red grid stops extractors AND crafters. The shop still launches if you remembered auto-launch.
Shredder sibling
T1/T2 extractors dump commons. Shredder Demands junk at priority −1 and auto-destroys when full
Keeps the pulsar line from clogging with iron. Recycle machine if you want the parts back.
